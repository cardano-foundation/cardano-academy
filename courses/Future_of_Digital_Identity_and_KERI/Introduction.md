# Introduction

Welcome everyone to the Cardano Academy. This course, The Future of Digital Identity and KERI, is an hour and a half long introductory training designed for enterprise users, decision-makers, and developers who want to understand the open standard redefining how digital identity works at its core. Our goal is to help you build verifiable trust to secure your digital future. By the end of this course, you should be able to understand the KERI protocol and see how real-world implementations like Veridian are putting these concepts into practice today. The course is structured into 18 units, each corresponding to a short video of approximately 5 minutes.

Throughout our time together, we will cover several core topics:

* The Web’s Missing Identity Layer.
* The Promise of Self-Sovereign Identity (SSI).
* KERI as a New Foundation for Trust.
* Autonomic Identifiers (AIDs).
* Veridian and Enterprise implementation.
* Authentic Chained Data Containers (ACDCs) and vLEIs.

We will explore how KERI acts as a "Trust Spanning Layer" to connect any application to any infrastructure. You will learn about "Pre-Rotation," a mechanism that protects your identity from theft by separating active signing keys from hidden rotation keys kept offline. We will also examine how ACDC chaining allows for precise delegation of authority, mirroring how real businesses operate. By completing this curriculum, you will gain a general summary of how Autonomic Trust offers a portable, secure, and scalable solution for the global internet.

By the end of this course, you should be able to:

* Explain the shift from "renting" to "owning" your digital life through SSI.
* Describe how an Identifier can be Self-Certifying and proven by math.
* Identify how Witnesses and Watchers detect duplicity to prevent fraud.
* Understand the vLEI hierarchy from GLEIF root to organizational roles.
